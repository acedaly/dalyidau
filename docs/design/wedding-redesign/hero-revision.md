# Hero revision — invitation first

## What was not working

The first PR #7 hero split the viewport into an ivory text panel and a large burgundy illustration panel. Its scale, hard division and flat vector florals made it read as a marketing landing page with a campaign graphic. The names were expressive but not truly stationery-like, while the dark botanical panel carried more visual weight than the invitation itself. On mobile, the two panels became separate stacked posters rather than one composed invitation.

## Revised direction

The hero now begins with a single portrait invitation on a quiet parchment field. A bespoke painted frame uses banksia, waratah, wattle, gum nuts and eucalyptus in the supplied chocolate, camel, burgundy, burnt-orange and botanical palette. Portal-managed photography remains supported as a soft atmospheric layer, but the paper invitation is always the primary object.

The couple's names use a restrained calligraphic face, supported by Cormorant Garamond for the date and Jost for practical details. The composition is centred, generous and deliberately print-like. Dark colour is reserved for ink and actions; the countdown and RSVP finale move back onto light paper so warm ivory remains dominant throughout the page.

## Design guardrails

- Native flora appears as a hero signature, not a repeated decoration in every section.
- Chocolate and espresso provide structure; burgundy, banksia orange, clay and wattle are accents.
- Content remains live HTML and continues to come from the portal.
- Hero media, section visibility, RSVP routing and all existing publishing behaviour are unchanged.
